A Tilted Binary Warps Spacetime Asymmetrically

Unequal masses orbit a shifted barycenter, while each body spins around its own tilted axis.

CONCEPTUAL BINARY / FRAME-DRAGGING / GRAVITATIONAL-WAVE VISUALIZATION
Brightness is warping. A line glows in proportion to how far it has been dragged from where it would sit in flat space. Color is ownership. Orange where the heavy body's frame-dragging twist wins, cyan where the light body's does, grey where neither reaches. The bands sweeping outward past the pair are the gravitational waves. A spike marks each body's spin axis, long end along its angular momentum. The two are not parallel, and the two bodies turn opposite ways. The faint line joining the pair runs through the barycenter at the small white cross, with the heavier body on the shorter arm.
